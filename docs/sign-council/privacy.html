Privacy Policy

Sign Council is an automated news-commentary program: five AI personas that read public technology & AI reporting and debate it on video, without a human host. This page covers the tools we use to publish that programming, including our TikTok posting integration.

EFFECTIVE 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 · v1.0

What this covers

This policy applies to Sign Council Poster, the publishing tool that posts Sign Council's own video episodes to our TikTok account using TikTok's Content Posting API. It does not cover TikTok itself, or how TikTok separately handles data for its viewers — see TikTok's own privacy policy for that.

What we access

  • An authentication token that lets our publishing tool post video files to our own, already-connected TikTok account — nothing more.
  • Standard delivery information TikTok returns after a post (for example, whether the upload succeeded), used only to confirm publishing.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect, read, or store data belonging to viewers of our TikTok videos — no follower lists, comments, messages, or profile information.
  • We do not use TikTok Login for anyone other than the Sign Council account owner authorizing this tool.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, and we run no advertising or tracking pixels through this integration.

In one sentence: this tool exists solely to publish Sign Council's own already-produced episodes to our own TikTok account — it is not a data-collection product.

Storage & retention

Authentication tokens are stored on infrastructure we control, used only to publish content, and deleted immediately if access is revoked from the TikTok account's settings or at our own request.

Children's privacy

Sign Council's programming is general technology & AI-industry commentary, not directed at children, and this tool does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Continued use of Sign Council's publishing tools after an update means you accept the revised policy.
